Ask about Lithuanian employment law. Get the article.

Answers come from the consolidated Darbo kodeksas, edition valid 2026-06-07 to 2026-10-31. Every answer cites a specific article — and when the Code doesn't cover your question, it says so instead of guessing.

⌘/Ctrl + Enter

Retrieval finds candidate articles by meaning, a cross-encoder reranks them, and a confidence gate decides whether there is enough support to answer at all. Citations are attached from the retriever, never written by the language model — so a source shown here is an article that was actually retrieved.

Not legal advice. A technical demonstration over one consolidated edition of one code. Verify anything that matters against e-TAR or a qualified professional. Method, measurements and limitations are in the repository. UI primitives adapted from Beautiful UI (MIT).

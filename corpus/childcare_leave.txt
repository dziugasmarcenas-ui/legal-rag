Leave to Raise a Child (Childcare Leave)
Labour Code of the Republic of Lithuania, Article 134

After pregnancy and childbirth leave ends, an employee is entitled to leave to raise a child until the child reaches three years of age. The leave may be taken by the mother, the father, the grandmother, the grandfather, or another relative actually raising the child, as well as by a guardian. It may be taken in full or in parts.

The persons entitled may take the leave in turns, but the leave cannot be used by more than one person at the same time, except where the law allows otherwise. The employee chooses the order and the parts of the leave and informs the employer in writing at least fourteen calendar days in advance.

During childcare leave the employer does not pay a salary. A childcare benefit is paid by the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra) for a defined period, subject to the required social insurance record. The duration and rate of the benefit depend on the option chosen by the family (for example, a benefit paid until the child is one year old at a higher rate, or until the child is two years old at a lower rate).

The employee's job position is retained during childcare leave. When the leave ends, the employee has the right to return to the same or an equivalent position on terms no less favourable, including any improvements in working conditions to which they would have been entitled.

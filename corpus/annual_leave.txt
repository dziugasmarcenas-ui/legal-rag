Annual Leave
Labour Code of the Republic of Lithuania, Articles 126-128

Employees are entitled to a minimum of 20 working days of paid annual leave where the employee works five days per week, or 24 working days where the employee works six days per week. Where the number of working days per week is lower or variable, the leave must not be shorter than four weeks.

Some employees are entitled to longer (extended) annual leave, for example employees under eighteen years of age, employees raising children, single parents, and employees with a reduced capacity for work. Additional annual leave may also be granted for long continuous service with the same employer, for work in particular conditions, and in other cases established by law or by the collective agreement.

Annual leave is granted during the working year. For the first working year, the right to the full leave usually arises after at least half of the working year has passed, although leave may be granted earlier by agreement. Unused annual leave is carried over and must generally not be accumulated indefinitely.

During annual leave the employee is paid holiday pay equal to their average wage. Holiday pay is paid no later than the last working day before the leave begins, unless the employee and employer agree otherwise. Replacing the minimum annual leave with a cash payment is not allowed while the employment relationship continues; unused leave is compensated in money only on termination of the employment contract.

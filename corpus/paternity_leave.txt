Paternity Leave
Labour Code of the Republic of Lithuania, Article 133

Working fathers are granted paternity leave of 30 calendar days. The leave may be taken at any time from the birth of the child until the child reaches one year of age. It may be used as one continuous period or, by agreement with the employer, split into parts.

Paternity leave is a separate entitlement from pregnancy and childbirth leave and from childcare leave. Taking paternity leave does not reduce the father's right to later take childcare leave to raise the child.

During paternity leave the employer does not pay a salary. A paternity benefit is paid by the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra), subject to the required social insurance record. The benefit replaces a percentage of the father's previous earnings.

The employee must notify the employer of the intended paternity leave in writing, normally at least ten working days in advance, unless a shorter period is agreed. The employee's job position is retained during the leave.

Pregnancy and Childbirth Leave (Maternity Leave)
Labour Code of the Republic of Lithuania, Article 132

Employees are granted pregnancy and childbirth leave of 126 calendar days in total: 70 calendar days before childbirth and 56 calendar days after childbirth. In the case of a complicated childbirth or the birth of more than one child, the leave after childbirth is extended, so that the total leave is increased by 14 calendar days.

The leave is calculated as a single combined period. If the employee does not use the full pre-birth portion, the unused days are not added to the post-birth portion; the post-birth leave remains 56 calendar days (or 70 calendar days in the case of a complicated birth or multiple children).

During pregnancy and childbirth leave the employee does not receive a salary from the employer. Instead, a maternity benefit is paid by the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra), provided the employee meets the required social insurance record. The benefit replaces a percentage of the employee's previous earnings.

The employee's position is protected during the leave. The employer may not terminate the employment contract with a pregnant employee, or with an employee on pregnancy and childbirth leave, except in a limited set of circumstances defined by law, such as the liquidation of the employer.

To take the leave, the employee provides the employer with a certificate of incapacity for work (issued for pregnancy and childbirth) generated in the electronic system. The leave begins on the date indicated in that certificate.
